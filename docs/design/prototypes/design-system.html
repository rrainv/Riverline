5 · Motion tokens

Four durations, four curves

Press Run. Every bar travels the same distance. Durations set how long; curves set the character. Table pace scales only the thinking and action beats, never interface feedback.

6 · Motion in use

One rule per kind of thing

Panel and view
Equity
Review

Old view fades and lifts 8px in dur-2 with ease-exit. New view rises 10px in dur-3 with ease-out. Never both at full opacity.

Rows

A new row drops in 10px and fades (dur-3, ease-out). Rows that must make room move by transform (FLIP), never by animating height. Removal is faster, with ease-exit. Order is stable: rows do not re-sort while you read.

Cards

Fly (dur-4, ease-out), then flip: the face swaps at the edge-on midpoint. Stagger 70ms. Folds leave face down with ease-exit.

Seats

The action ring travels seat to seat with ease-inout in dur-3 × pace. Thinking dots loop until the bot acts. A seat pops once with ease-spring when it wins.

Menus and overlays

Scrim fades in dur-2. The surface scales from 0.96 and rises in dur-3 with ease-out. Closing takes dur-2. Focus moves in at once, not after the animation.

Numbers and bars
25.6%

Bars grow from the reading-start edge with scaleX in dur-4 (transform only, no width animation). The number counts in the same time and lands exactly on the engine value. No overshoot on data: springs are for objects, not facts.
