The Lamp · expressions in motion

One light, eight moods

All animated · no sound
IdleBreathes, blinks now and then. Always present, never loud.“Good evening. Ten minutes on your big blind?”
ThinkingCool light, a slow orbit, eyes drifting. Shown while the engine works.“Let me look at the hands you folded.”
CuriousBeam tilts, one brow up. Used when asking you a question.“Why did you call there?”
SpeakingRings radiate while a line is shown. Quiet by default.“Here is what I would change tonight.”
ApprovingWider, brighter beam and a small spark. A leak closed.“That is a leak closed. Three this month.”
CelebratingRare: levels and achievements. A short burst, then back to idle.“Level 7. You earned that.”
SternBeam narrows and cools. Only when the maths earns the word.“You called off 100 bb needing 45% with 26%. That one is on you.”
RestingDim and slow when you are away or paused. Wakes on a click.“…”
