# Playtest report: hands-on user and product feel (2026-11-21)

**Setup:** `main` at `f9f98d8`, `npm run dev` (Vite 6.4.6), Claude desktop built-in browser emulating **1366×768**. Default theme (Midnight), then Paper, Daylight, Hebrew and Russian. I used the mouse and keyboard throughout. `__riverlineTest` was never used. In the duel, hands 3–10 were sped up by clicking the app's own buttons from the console after hands 1–2 had been played by hand. No application code was edited. All local data was erased through Settings at the end to test empty states.

**What this is:** observations from one session, not acceptance. Gates were not re-run (`tsc`, tests, golden and audits were reported green in the handoff).

**One exact-maths check:** A♠K♦ vs K♣Q♣ on K♥7♣2♦. I enumerated all 990 runouts with a standalone evaluator: hero wins 82.12%, ties 1.21%, loses 16.67%, so equity is 82.7%. Equity room matches. Review/Teacher text does not (see B3).

**Not reached:** a 14×14 villain range painter in Equity, the duel archetypes, and the Lamp's mood changes (only the idle face was seen; no twitchiness, but no celebrate/warn/teach was observed either). Each is noted below.

Format per item: **Room/Flow · Steps · What happened · Why it feels off · Direction.** IDs map to `BUG_INBOX.md` rows #17 onward.

## 1. [BROKEN / BUG]

### B1 (P1, money): Receipt says everyone finished even while the ledger is off and someone lost ₪350
- **Room/Flow:** Nights → Settle.
- **Steps:** Start a night (₪, 4 players, 200/200/100/300). Rebuy Oren +₪150. Cash out Viktor 450, Oren 0, Maya 180, Ilya 300 (₪20 short). Open Settle.
- **What happened:** The left panel correctly says "The ledger is off by ₪20". The right-hand Morning-after summary shows "Transfers 0" with the badge "Exact · ledger balance exact". The receipt text reads *"All players finished even. Zero transfers needed."* Copy receipt and Download .txt are enabled. Oren is −₪350 in the standings directly above.
- **Why off:** This is a false money statement, one click away from being pasted into the group chat.
- **Direction:** While the ledger is unbalanced, don't build a receipt. Show "Not balanced yet: off by ₪20", disable Copy and Download, and drop the "ledger balance exact" badge.

### B2 (P1, money units): Quick-logged hand pot in bb is shown as shekels
- **Room/Flow:** Nights → Hands quick logger, Settle "Hands from tonight", receipt.
- **Steps:** Shekel night. Log `co r6 btn c bb c / Qs 9h 4c bb x co b10 btn c bb f / 2d co x btn x / Jc co b25 btn f` at 50 bb.
- **What happened:** The row reads `BTN Q♥J♥ · … · ₪38.5`. 38.5 is the pot in **bb** (18.5 + 20). The same "₪38.5" goes into the copied receipt.
- **Why off:** It's a wrong currency on a money surface. The shorthand amounts (`r6`, `b10`) have no unit either.
- **Direction:** Label quick-logger amounts as bb (or convert with the night's blinds), and show hero's result, not the pot.

### B3 (P1, false number): Review and Teacher call a 3-way equity "against the revealed hand"
- **Room/Flow:** Review → Decision 2 (flop call).
- **Steps:** Record UTG raise, BTN (hero A♠K♦) call, BB call. Flop K♥7♣2♦: BB check, UTG bet, hero call, BB fold. Reveal UTG K♣Q♣. Open Review, Decision 2, Showed hand.
- **What happened:** The rows show "You had 74.8%" and "Opponent (UTG) 16.4%", which add up to 91.2. The Teacher says *"Hero held about 74.8% equity against the revealed hand (Kc Qc) across 10,000 sampled runouts. Because preflop equity is sampled…"*. Against K♣Q♣ alone the exact figure is **82.7%**: the Equity room shows "Exact · all 990 runouts 82.7%", which matches my enumeration. 74.8 is a 3-way number that includes the still-live BB (8.8%), and the BB has no row. The Teacher also says "preflop" on a flop decision.
- **Why off:** It's a wrong claim in prose next to a number. Rows that don't add to 100 make a player go "wait, who has the other 9%?"
- **Direction:** Add the live BB as a row ("BB · unknown cards · 8.8%"), word the Teacher line as "against UTG's K♣Q♣ and the BB's unknown hand", and fix the street in the template.

### B4 (P1, honesty): A leak with a points score appears after one drill answer that was judged "Aligned"
- **Room/Flow:** Training → Quick drill → Training hub.
- **Steps:** Answer one Quick drill (BTN 53s, Fold). The Teacher says "Aligned with the estimated baseline mix (70%)". Open Training → Modes.
- **What happened:** "This week → Top spots to drill: **BTN · Suited one-gappers (−83 pts)**".
- **Why off:** It contradicts the verdict a second earlier. It's built from one drill answer (AGENTS §4: no stats from drills, show "watching" below the evidence threshold), and "pts" reads like a mastery score.
- **Direction:** Below threshold, show "watching (1)" or nothing. Never derive a leak from drill answers, and drop "pts".

### B5 (P1, data): Delete all data doesn't fully clear, then starter hands reappear and feed your stats
- **Room/Flow:** Settings → Data → Delete all data → visit rooms.
- **What happened:** (a) Without a reload, Review still shows the deleted A♠K♦ hand (+₪21.5), the Saved library badge still says 2, and Equity still holds the old matchup. (b) After a reload, the library holds 2 "Starter example" hands, pre-tagged "Study", and the Assumption Mirror counts "Semi-bluff or draw · Watching · n = 2" from them.
- **Why off:** Right after "erase everything", my deleted data is still visible. Then example data I never played counts as my own assumptions.
- **Direction:** Reset in-memory stores (or reload) on erase. Keep starter examples out of every statistic, and don't re-seed after an explicit erase (or ask first).

### B6 (P2): Enter, Space and J/K do nothing in the flows that need them most
- **Room/Flow:** Hand recorder, Quick drill, Heads-up duel, Review.
- **Steps and what happened:**
  - Recorder: press `F` (Fold is selected), then `Enter`: nothing is recorded.
  - Quick drill: `1` selects, but `Enter` and `Space` neither confirm nor move to the next spot (focus is on `<body>`).
  - Duel: same as the drill.
  - Review: `J`, `K` and the arrow keys don't step, whether focus is on `<main>` or after clicking a decision row.
  - Settings → Shortcuts lists none of these (only Ctrl+K, Shift+F, Ctrl+,, E, F, C, R, A).
- **Why off:** At 1 AM, "letter then Enter" is the whole promise of the keyboard tickets.
- **Direction:** Enter = confirm the selected ticket, Space/Enter = Next in drills, J/K = step in Review. List all of them in Shortcuts.

### B7 (P2): Hero's action bubble covers the board cards
- **Room/Flow:** Hand recorder, any postflop street.
- **What happened:** Hero's "Call" and "Check" bubbles sit on top of the flop/turn cards (zoom confirmed overlap with the 7♣ and 2♦).
- **Direction:** Anchor hero's bubble beside or below hero's seat, or nudge it up above the board.

### B8 (P2, money): The ledger side panel's primary button is "Cash out ₪0"
- **Room/Flow:** Nights → Ledger → row "Rebuy" (or "Cash out").
- **What happened:** Both row buttons open the same panel. Its eyebrow says "REBUY" even when you opened it with "Cash out". The accent-filled primary button is "Cash out ₪0", so one stray click busts a player at zero. The rebuy default is ₪100 regardless of the player's buy-in (Oren bought in for ₪200), and rebuys can only be stepped in ₪50 increments, never typed. The cash-out field isn't focused automatically and Enter doesn't submit. After cashing out, the row still offers "Rebuy" and "Cash out".
- **Direction:** Open the panel focused on the action that was clicked, with that action as primary. Make rebuy default to the player's last buy-in and accept typed amounts. After a cash-out, show "Edit cash-out".

### B9 (P2): Card picker problems
- **Room/Flow:** Nights quick logger, Hand recorder showdown, inline board picker.
- **What happened:**
  - In Game night the confirm button reads **"Save to plan"**.
  - The picker closes after card 1, so you reopen it for card 2.
  - Suits in the modal picker all render the same off-white (only the selected card is jade), with no suit colour.
  - Rank is "T" in the modal but "10" in the inline board picker.
  - The aria label reads "A of spades".
- **Direction:** Context-specific confirm label (or auto-close after card 2), suit colours from the card-face setting, one rank glyph, and "Ace of spades".

### B10 (P2): Context is lost between rooms
- **Night → recorder:** the quick logger had Q♥J♥; "Record full hand" opens with the default A♠K♦.
- **Review → "Drill this"** on a flop call: opens an unrelated **preflop** drill (BTN 53s, 100 bb, 6-max).
- **Review → "Open in Equity":** the BB is dropped (heads-up 82.7/17.3), and "Can I call?" shows defaults of 12 bb / 6 bb / 40 bb instead of the hand's ₪9.5 pot, ₪5 bet and ₪44 stack.
- **Direction:** Pass the full decision state (players live, pot, bet, stacks, street) on every jump.

### B11 (P2): Equity players can't be edited or removed
- **Room/Flow:** Equity, starting from two rows.
- **What happened:** Clicking hero's cards does nothing, and there is no edit or remove control with two players. A "×" only appears once there are 3+ rows, and it's clipped at the panel's right edge. Setting up "A♥Q♥ vs a range on K♠T♦4♥" from an existing matchup isn't possible.
- **Direction:** Click a row's cards to edit them, and always show remove (with at least one opponent kept).

### B12 (P2): Settings visual bugs
- The **Midnight swatch repaints itself in the active theme's colours** (cream and brown under Paper, white and green under Daylight).
- The page header stays "APPEARANCE / Appearance for this device" on every tab (Language, Shortcuts, Data…).

### B13 (P2, RTL): Notation breaks in Hebrew
- **Room/Flow:** Hand recorder toolbar in `he`.
- **What happened:** `6-max · 100 bb · 0.5/1 bb` renders as `max · 100 bb · 0.5/1 bb-6`. The Home game chip is reordered to `bb ₪0.5/₪1 · 50 · ערב פוקר`. The Language preview `רייז ל-7.5 bb עם AhKh` puts the "ל-" on the wrong side of the number.
- **Direction:** Wrap each preset fragment in `<bdi dir="ltr">`, and isolate the number plus unit as one token.

### B14 (P2, ru): Russian overflow and untranslated strings
- "Экви[ти] E" is clipped at the toolbar's right edge, and the rail item is truncated to "Сохранённые р…".
- "Hero" is left untranslated ("Позиция Hero", "Карты Hero"), and "сбросить до Него" capitalises "Него".

### B15 (P2): Duel seats and the match report
- After a hand settles, Astra's label flips to "SB" while hero still shows "BTN / SB", so both are SB until the next deal.
- The match report shows *"Hand #1 · 9♣6♣ vs 5♠2♥"*: Astra's cards from a hand she folded preflop. Key moments reveal her cards in hands hero folded (K♥4♥, A♦8♦). Review's own rule is "Opponent cards stay face down before showdown to avoid hindsight leakage".
- **Direction:** Pick one policy. If folded cards are shown for study, label them "revealed after the hand".

### B16 (P2): Recorder end state and the save toolbar
- After showdown the felt shows "POT 0 ₪", the River header says "Pot ₪0", "Hand settled" appears twice, and the Tap/Typed toggle is still visible.
- After "Save hand", "✓ Save hand" plus "Saved" plus "Open in Review" appear, then vanish after about 2 s and the toolbar reflows. My click on "Back to tonight's game" landed on empty space.
- **Direction:** Keep the saved state stable, keep the pot visible ("Hero collected ₪39.5"), and remove the duplicates.

### B17 (P2): Shorthand error messages mislead

## 2. [FEEL / FRICTION]

**F1. Logging a hand at 1 AM is tedious.**
- **Recorder:** every villain action is select then confirm (about 30 clicks for an ordinary hand), Enter doesn't confirm (B6), opening sizes pushes "Record action" below the fold at 1366×768, and the page scroll jumps after each action.
- **Night quick logger:** fast and good, but it has no result field, and stack depth stops at 100 bb (no 150/200/custom).
- **Direction:** Quick-tap mode where one tap records villain fold/check/call, and confirm is kept only for hero and for sizing.

**F2. The table can't be described as it really was.**
- Table rules have no blind-size control (my 1/2 game is forced to ₪0.5/₪1).
- Per-seat stack chips cycle +10 on click, with no way to decrease or type a value.

**F3. Four shorthand dialects** (against "one shorthand grammar"):
- Quick logger: `btn r12 sb f…`
- Recorder typed: `f f r2.5 r8 f f c / Qh7c2h`
- Saved summary: `r3 f f c f c / Kh7c2d x b5 c f`
- Handoff: `AhKd BTN 2.5bb, BB call | Flop…`

**F4. "Why did I lose that?"**
- The recorder says "Showdown winner: BTN".
- The duel says "Astra wins pot (-2 bb)", with no hand names and no visible winning-hand outline.
- On a paired board (6♥7♠T♣9♠9♦) I had to work out myself that Astra's 6 made two pair.

**F5. The receipt isn't group-chat ready.**
- No date, ASCII "->", and no per-player net.
- It includes hero's hand lines ("BTN A♠K♦ · … · ₪39.5"), which mean nothing to the table and carry B2's unit error.

**F6. Real home games are often off by a chip.** Settle is hard-blocked at ₪20 off, and the only remedy is to edit entries. An explicit "record ₪20 as lost / split the shortfall" step would match reality.

**F7. Duplication and noise everywhere:**
- Rail and top tabs duplicate each other (Nights, Settings).
- "Still on the table ₪800" appears twice.
- "Exact · ledger balance exact" sits next to a second "Exact" chip.
- Eyebrow repeats the heading: "ESTIMATED BASELINE MIX / Estimated baseline mix / Estimated" and "SHOW THE LAMP / Show the Lamp".
- "Watching · n = 0" sits on every Assumption Mirror pill.
- "Unknown, not fold" ×8, and "not asked" in every Strategy cell plus "Not asked yet".
- Equity shows the hand-class breakdown twice and "wins N of 903 rivers" twice per row.
- "(no rake)" appears 4 times on Nights.

**F8. Spec-speak and placeholder copy:**
- "Live buy-ins, cash-out balance gate, fewest-transfers settlement"
- "Equity by rows / Stable seat order with full runouts"
- "IndexedDB"
- "Voluntary Check on River"
- "Open Friday example" in a real hand's header
- "Selected hand" and "Selected size:" (B16 copy rule)
- "2 bb (2 bb)" in the sizing text

**F9. The Teacher reads like a template.**
- I picked "Bluff catching" for a top-pair-top-kicker call, and the verdict ignored it.
- It closes on meta text ("Riverline does not call this a math mistake") and uses ASCII "Kc Qc".
- A smart friend would say: "You weren't bluff-catching, you were way ahead: KQ only had about 17%."

**F10. Daylight reads as cool grey-white SaaS.** Paper is the warm-craft one. Empty-seat card slots render as heavy dark blocks on the light felt.

**F11. Duel:**
- Only Astra; no archetype choice (Calling Station / Pressure Regular aren't in the UI).
- "New 10-hand duel" silently discards the running match.
- Stacks reset to about 99/101 every hand while "Match net −26 bb" accumulates.
- Key moments are just the three biggest losses, with no "why".
- The "Hand action" panel is an empty box before the first action.
- Check is `C` here but `X` in the recorder.
- **Pacing is good at Normal:** the bot acts about 1 s after you and the next street lands about 1.4 s later, which is trackable.

**F12. Equity runout labels confuse:**
- When ahead, blanks are counted as "Upgrades (+5%+): 22", while "What this card does to you: Blank −3.8%".
- Hero's "+12.4%" delta has no stated baseline.
- Blocker X-Ray counts against "any two" (1176 → 1081) rather than the selected villain range, and uses ASCII "->".
- No range painter grid was reachable.

**F13. Learn:** the Quick trainer asks the exact worked-example numbers (12 bb pot, 6 bb bet, 25%) right below the example. "Continue with Equity" is primary while Pot odds is still "In progress".

**F14. Settings gaps against locked A3.11 and the handoff:**
- No heading-font choice, no density, no four-colour deck (card faces are Ivory/White/Slate).
- Two teacher voices (Blunt/Gentle) instead of three.
- Teacher typing speed sits under Table & motion, not Teacher.
- "Reduced motion" and "Minimal card animation" overlap.
- No sound controls.

**F15. Units:** only Chips and ₪ (the handoff expects $ / ₪ / ₽). Prefilled values "Eli, Dan, Noa" and "Friday at Dan's" are real values, not placeholders, so a hurried user starts a night with fake players.

**F16. Recorder oddities:**
- Bot "thinking" dots on seats in a manual recorder.
- "Everyone else folds to you" is still offered after hero has acted.
- "To act: UTG (UTG)".
- The focus ring looks identical to the selected state on action tickets (Fold "looks selected" next to Raise).
- The preflop open is described as "60% of pot".
- The "Saved hands" rail item jumps to Review's library and switches the dock.

**F17. Home empty state** shows 0/0/0 plus an empty 7-day strip (A2.13: no reason, so the region should shrink).

## 3. [HIGH-LEVERAGE POLISH]

1. **Enter confirms, Space/Enter goes Next, J/K steps** wherever a ticket is selected, all listed in Shortcuts. Fixes B6 and most of F1.
2. **Gate the receipt on balance** (disable Copy/Download, show "off by ₪20"). Fixes B1 in a few lines.
3. **Real placeholders, not prefilled names.** Use `inputmode="decimal"` on amounts, autofocus the cash-out field, make Enter submit, and default rebuys to the last buy-in.
4. **One "Save & back to tonight's game" button**, with a stable saved state and no toolbar reflow.
5. **Hand names at showdown** ("Two pair, nines and sixes beats king high"), in the recorder, Review and the duel.
6. **Carry state on every jump** (hero cards from the night logger, the same street and spot for Drill this, live players and pot for Open in Equity).
7. **Show hero's result (+/−) on logged-hand rows** instead of the pot.
8. **Add the missing live-player row** in Review equity so rows add up to 100%.
9. **Receipt v2:** date, nets, transfers, and the Unicode "→"; hands off by default.
10. **Request persistent storage automatically** the first time money is entered, and drop "IndexedDB" from copy.
11. **Static theme swatches**, and a Settings header that follows the active tab.
12. **One rank glyph (T or 10) and suit colours** in every card picker.
