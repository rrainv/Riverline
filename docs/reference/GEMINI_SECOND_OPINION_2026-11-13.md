# Riverline second opinion

## 0. Summary: the five most important things (one line each)

1. **[Verified] False "Exact" math in the mockups breaks the brand promise:** Screen `14` claims `"Astra needs 30% to call · SPR after a call 7.5 · Exact · price only"` on a half-pot bet (`2.5 bb` into `5 bb`, `97.5 bb` behind) when true required equity is `25.0%` and post-call SPR is `9.5` (`95/10`), while Screen `06` calls turn `J♠` `"A blank"` for `A♥K♥` on `Q♥ 7♣ 2♥` despite `J♠` adding 3 Broadway gutshot outs (`T♠, T♦, T♣`) for `10/44 = 22.7%`.
2. **[Opinion] Separating `Your Plan`, `Your Dealt Play`, and `Tiered Baseline` is a genuine wedge, but bad baseline spots kill trust:** Screen `04` shows the baseline folding `A♠K♠` `10%` and flat-calling `40%` on the Button facing a `2.5 bb` Cutoff open (`100 bb` deep).
3. **[Opinion] "Port mockup HTML + pixel-diff gate" will recreate the 13.6k-line maintenance collapse:** Static `1440×900` English mockup markup breaks under live engine numbers, `1366×768` viewports, `200–300%` Russian string growth, and Hebrew RTL; build 12 shared Preact primitives instead.
4. **[Observed] Three simultaneous navigation bars (`Top Bar` + `Left Room Rail` + `Bottom Floating Dock`) and the "table as a menu" (`02`, `03`) waste 28–50% of the viewport:** Collapse chrome to one primary bar and reserve the poker table strictly for active hands.
5. **[Opinion] Scope is ~3× too wide for a 30-day self-test:** Cut XP/levels, 5-bot multiway play, the 8-mood/6-theme Lamp matrix, and the standalone Sunday Letter room to ship the core `Game Night → Review → Leak Map → Quick Drill` loop.

## 1. Product critique (4.1)

### 1.1 Single strongest idea vs. weakest assumption

* **Strongest idea — Triangulating *Intention* (`Your Plan`), *Execution* (`Your Dealt Play`), and *Reference* (`Tiered Baseline`) [Opinion]:** Mainstream trainers ([GTO Wizard](https://gtowizard.com/), [DTO Poker](https://www.dtopoker.com/), [PeakGTO](https://pokercoaching.com/pricing/)) grade players against Nash equilibrium mixes [Verified]. Recreational players follow a simplified personal plan and deviate under pressure [Opinion]. Separating **Plan vs. Baseline** (*"Is my plan sound?"*) from **Play vs. Plan** (*"Did I execute my own plan?"*, as in `01_home_coach.png` where the plan defends 18 BB hands but dealt play folded 14) [Observed] is the sharpest pedagogical hook in the pack.
* **Weakest assumption — Blank-slate plan entry and manual transcription clearing `n ≥ 15` dealt hands per spot [Opinion]:** `RIVERLINE_FOUNDING_DRAFT.md` (§7) and `NORTH_STAR.md` (§7.1) delay templates so users build their plan via question flows and matrix painting [Observed]. A 6-max preflop plan spans thousands of cells [Verified], causing immediate cold-start fatigue [Opinion]. Meanwhile, a live home game deals ~25–30 hands/hour (~4–5 BB hands/hour) [Verified]; reaching `n = 15` dealt hands in one specific spot (`BB vs. BTN open`) takes 4–6 weeks even if every live hand is transcribed [Verified].

### 1.3 Teacher voice, real user profile, and 5 things to postpone

* **Teacher voice [Opinion]:** Strong when separating variance from skill (`15`: *"+21.5 bb: Raw result. Ten hands say almost nothing about skill"*), but risky where it uses showdown hindsight to blame the player (`07`, `21`) or repeats defensive caveats in prose (`13`, `15`: *"so I am watching it, not judging it"*), violating `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_V2.md` §8 (*"Quiet copy"*) [Observed].
* **Who is the user & why they quit in Week 2 [Opinion]:** Casual home players don't study. Your real user is the **Analytical Home-Game Host / University & Tech Reg** (`10k–50k` organic niche globally) who runs the Friday ledger and wants study tools without paying `$49–$206/mo` for [GTO Wizard](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2026/03/gto-wizard-subscription-plans-new-features-pricing-50908.htm) [Verified]. They will quit in Week 2 if forced to: (1) manually tap 15+ live hands from memory, (2) stare at `"Watching (4/15)"` for a month, or (3) trust a baseline that folds `AKs` on the Button (`04`).
* **Five things to postpone for M1 [Opinion]:** (1) `Full Hand vs. 5 Bots` (`16`); (2) 8-mood × 6-theme Lamp sheets (`21`, `22`); (3) XP, `Level 6`, and Stickers (`01`); (4) Standalone `Weekly Teacher Letter` room (`17`, `18`); (5) Separate `Tap` (`09`) and `Shorthand` (`10`) recorder modes (merge into one).
* **Top 3 actions (Section 1):** (1) Grade `Review` decisions (`07`) against Villain's *estimated range*, reserving `"Exact · known cards"` for all-in luck; (2) Seed `Personal Strategy` (`05`) from a modifiable preset; (3) Cut XP, 5-bot play, and the standalone Letter room.

### 2.2 Differentiation, commodities, and legal/platform risk

* **Genuine differentiation vs. commodity traps [Opinion]:** Riverline genuinely differs via (1) `Plan vs. Play vs. Baseline`, (2) the **Friday Ledger (`19`) → Hand Capture (`20`) → Saturday Review (`07`)** loop, and (3) honest confidence tiers (`Exact` vs. `Estimated`) in `EN/RU/HE (RTL)`. Conversely, building **range-vs-range matrix heatmaps in Equity** (`RIVERLINE_FOUNDING_DRAFT.md` §8) or **training custom postflop neural nets** (`NORTH_STAR.md` §3.4) rebuilds commoditised features owned by [Flopzilla Pro](https://www.flopzilla.com/) and [GTO Wizard](https://gtowizard.com/) [Verified].
* **Online RTA bans [Verified]:** [GGPoker's Security & Ecology Policy](https://www.yourpokerdream.com/overview-dealspartner/comparison-pokerstars-vs-ggpoker/) bans all charts, solvers, and third-party hand converters during play, and [GGPoker partnered with GTO Wizard in March 2025](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2025/03/ggpoker-and-gto-wizard-team-up-to-keep-poker-fair-48110.htm) to detect concurrent browser study lookups [Verified]. [PokerStars](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2020/10/what-is-meant-by-real-time-assistance-rta-38054.htm) enforces strict process bans [Verified]. *Action:* Never read screen pixels or active logs, and warn users to close Riverline during live online play [Opinion].
* **Israeli law (`Penal Law 5737-1977 §224`) & RU/HE store risk [Verified]:** Israeli statute bans organizing games where chance predominates; the Supreme Court's 2018 *Rafiach* tax ruling recognized tournament skill in *obiter dictum*, leaving lower-court cash precedents untouched ([Chambers Gaming Law 2025](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/comparison/953/17713/27682-27683-27684-27685-27686-27687-27688-27689-27690-27691-27692-27693); [Herzog Fox & Neeman](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/news-and-insights/israels-conservative-stance-on-gambling-legal-framework-exceptions-and-emerging-trends/)) [Verified]. *Action:* Default `Game night` (`19`) to unitless `chips / pts` or `bb` rather than `₪` cash settlement, never include rake fields, and never process payments [Opinion].
* **Top 3 actions (Section 2):** (1) Default `Game night` (`19`) to unitless `pts / bb` instead of `₪`; (2) Add an RTA warning to close Riverline during live online play; (3) Drop range-vs-range heatmaps from M1/M2 Equity.

## 4. Motion and interaction (4.4)

* **Easing & duration flaw (`base ÷ tempo`) [Verified]:** `MOTION_SPEC.md` defines `real duration = base ÷ tempo` (`Snappy 1.0`, `Balanced 0.7`, `Fluid 0.5`) [Observed]. At `0.7` or `0.5`, Moment 1 (`900 ms ÷ 0.7 = 1,286 ms`), Moment 3 (`+900 ms ÷ 0.7 = 1,286 ms` delay before reason text appears), Moment 6 (`860 ms ÷ 0.7 = 1,228 ms` flop deal), and Moment 27 (`1,100 ms ÷ 0.6 = 1,833 ms`) turn drill loops into sluggish >1.2s waits, contradicting `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_V2.md` §7.1 (`150–350 ms`) [Verified].
* **Accessibility & weak-laptop risks [Verified/Opinion]:**
  1. *Vestibular & reduced motion:* Moment 10 (169-cell radial wave) and Moment 22 (`700 ms` circular reveal) risk motion discomfort. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, collapse all spatial slides, ripples, 3D flips, and odometer rolls to `≤150 ms` opacity fades while keeping a `150–200 ms` pause between bot actions.
  2. *Screen readers & keyboard parity:* Mark rolling digit reels (Moments 4, 18) `aria-hidden="true"` with a single parent `aria-label` so screen readers don't read intermediate ticks; replace hover-only price tooltips (`14`) with a focus/tap-accessible inline bar.

## 6. Teaching and trust (4.6)

* **Leak Scan (`11`–`13`) critique & replacement [Opinion]:** Asking 6 isolated `"Always / Sometimes / Never"` questions (`"Do you open Q9o from UTG?"`) measures self-report bias, not play, and extrapolating an entire `"Offsuit queens"` gap from `n = 1` question is indefensible. **Replace with a 5-Spot Interactive Threshold & Concept Calibration:** (1) Drag a cutoff slider on `Suited Kings` (`AKs → K2s`) from `UTG (5 behind)` and show exact domination probability; (2) Drag the cutoff on `Suited Connectors` from `BTN (2 behind)`; (3) `BB vs. BTN 2.5 bb open` pot-odds check with `Q♠7♠` (`27.3%` price); (4) `BTN vs. CO` 3-bet bluff choice (`A♦4♦` blocker vs. dominated `K♣J♦`); (5) Turn half-pot call (`25.0%` price) with a 9-out flush draw (`19.6%`) to teach implied odds.
* **Evidence gate (`n ≥ 15`) critique & replacement [Verified/Opinion]:** A flat `n = 15` is statistically arbitrary: opening `Q9o` UTG 4 of 5 times against a `2%` baseline has binomial **`p = 0.000008`** (obvious at `n = 5`), whereas `10 of 15` on a `50%` mixed c-bet has **`p = 0.302`** (noise even at `n = 15`) [Verified]. Worse, manually recorded live hands suffer from ~90% VPIP selection bias. **Rule:** Flag `"Noticed"` when the **90% Beta-Binomial credible interval** (`Beta(1,1)` prior) falls entirely outside the target tolerance band with **`n ≥ 5`**, and compute frequency stats **only** from full-deal streams (`Duel`, `Full Hand`, full imports).
* **Skill-acquisition science & 4 ways "honesty" misleads [Verified/Opinion]:**
  * Immediate feedback in `Quick Drill` (`04`, [Anderson et al., 1989](https://www.chessflow.org/science)) + delayed feedback in `Duel` (`14/15`, [Schmidt & Bjork, 1992](https://www.chessflow.org/science)) is sound, but placing `"WHAT WERE YOU THINKING?"` (`07`) *after* `"Minus EV. A mistake"` breaks the **Self-Explanation Effect** ([Chi et al., 1989](https://www.chessflow.org/science))—ask *before* revealing the verdict. Replace fixed `"24h Revisit"` with **FSRS-5 (`ts-fsrs`)**, cutting prediction RMSE in half (`0.102` vs. `0.203`) and review load by **20–30%** ([Azri Oct 2026](https://azri.app/fsrs-vs-sm2/); [Imprimo Oct 2026](https://imprimo.app/blog/fsrs-vs-sm2-why-your-flashcard-app-uses-outdated-algorithm)) [Verified].
  * **Four ways "honesty" misleads:** (1) Showing `"Exact · price only: 38.5%"` preflop/flop (`04`, `14`) baits out-of-position calls that ignore poor equity realization (`EQR`) at `SPR 19.5`; (2) Hindsight `"Exact · known cards"` grading (`07`) shames good range calls that ran into top set; (3) `1 pt` integer deltas (`+41`, `-3`) next to `Estimated` (`05`) fake precision; (4) Comparing 40 bb no-rake multiway home play to a 100 bb raked 6-max baseline (`05`).
* **Top 3 actions (Section 6):** (1) Replace the 6-question scan with the 5-Spot Threshold Calibration; (2) Use a Beta-Binomial 90% credible interval (`n ≥ 5`) on full-deal streams only; (3) Prompt self-explanation *before* revealing verdicts and schedule repeats with `ts-fsrs`.
